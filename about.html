About Lockwood Interiors

Bringing nature into the spaces people build, sell and live in.

Lockwood Interiors supplies and installs premium wood flooring, decking, cladding and surface finishes across Delhi NCR. We work directly with homeowners as well as the architects, interior designers, builders and contractors who bring their projects to life — and with retail teams fitting out new stores.

Every product that leaves our workshop is measured, cut and installed by our own team. We don't hand the finish off to a third party, which is how we hold the same standard whether the job is a single living room or a full commercial fit-out.

How We Work

From site visit to handover

A genuine sequence — each step depends on the one before it.

01 · Brief & site visit

We measure the space and understand use, footfall and finish preferences.

02 · Samples & spec

You choose from our finish library; we confirm technical specs and BOQ.

03 · Supply

Material is sourced and delivered in phases that match your site schedule.

04 · Installation

Our own crews install, finish and clear the site.

05 · Handover & care

Walkthrough, maintenance guidance, and warranty documentation.
